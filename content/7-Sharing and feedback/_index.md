### Overall Impressions and Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
During my internship, I found the working environment friendly, open, and supportive of interns taking the initiative to learn. I could focus on my assigned tasks while also having room to explore AWS topics I had never worked with before. What I valued most was that the environment did not put too much pressure on me, yet still expected everyone to be self-disciplined and responsible for their own work.

**2. Support from Mentors and Team Admin**  
I ran into quite a few Cloud-related problems, as well as some unrelated ones, and the mentors and admins always helped me enthusiastically. In particular, the Cloud knowledge they shared during the workshop sessions and while I was working on the workshop taught me that it is not enough to make a system work. I also need to explain why I chose a particular service and how the services connect to each other. As a result, I gradually improved the way I think about and present a technical solution.

**3. Fit Between the Actual Work and My Major**  
I am majoring in **Computer Networks and Communications**, so before joining the program my knowledge mostly covered networking, network administration, and some systems knowledge. When I started learning AWS, I saw that Cloud has many topics related to what I had already studied, such as servers, storage, access control, networking, and system monitoring, but the way they are implemented in practice has many new aspects.

While working on the project **"Building a Backup & Recovery System for a Web Server on AWS"**, I had the chance to combine my server and networking knowledge with Cloud services such as EC2, EBS, S3, IAM, AWS Backup, and CloudWatch. This helped me better understand how what I learned at school can be applied in a Cloud environment.

**4. Opportunities for Learning and Personal Growth**  
The internship let me learn a lot that I had not studied in depth before, especially Cloud services. While working on the project, I did not just follow instructions. I also had to research documentation, debug errors, and try several different configurations before getting the result I wanted.

Through this process, I improved my ability to learn on my own, find documentation, analyze problems, and verify results after each deployment step. I also realized that I need to understand the essence of each service rather than just memorizing the steps in the AWS Console.

**5. Company Culture and Teamwork**  
I felt the working environment values initiative and self-learning. Each intern is responsible for their own progress, but when facing difficulties, they can still discuss them and get support.

This helped me gradually build the habit of investigating a problem on my own first, then gathering the parts I could not solve to discuss with others. In addition, learning and exchanging ideas with fellow interns gave me new perspectives on solving problems and motivated me while completing the Workshop.

**6. Policies and Benefits for Interns**  
During the internship, I was given the opportunity to learn new knowledge while practicing directly on AWS. I appreciate that the program does not only require completing a project, but also includes sharing sessions, guidance, and reviews that help interns better understand how real work is done.

Gaining access to Cloud knowledge, practicing directly with AWS services, and attending knowledge-sharing sessions while working on the project were valuable experiences for me. Through this internship, I have a clearer direction for continuing to study AWS, Cloud Computing, and topics related to my major in Computer Networks and Communications.

As a coffee lover, I especially enjoyed the coffee corner at the office, which gave me plenty of energy and kept me alert for studying.
